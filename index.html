The tech I can’t leave alone.

What keeps pulling me in.

  • Phones that act like computers

    Termux, Debian, QEMU, a Linux desktop in my pocket. I like finding out how far a phone can really be pushed.

  • Linux & servers

    A terminal, a blinking cursor, and a machine that does exactly what I tell it. I love taking a bare server and slowly turning it into something that just works.

  • Apps & browsers

    I can lose a whole evening working out how a favourite app behaves, what an extension really does, and why one page loads in a blink while another crawls. The small details matter to me.

  • Learning by trying

    I don’t wait until I understand everything. I break it, read the error, fix it, and the lesson sticks far better than any manual.

  • How things really work

    Weather, big numbers, countries, the scale of the universe. I follow a question until it finally makes sense.
